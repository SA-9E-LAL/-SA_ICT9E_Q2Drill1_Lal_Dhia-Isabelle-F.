Beginner's Guide to Volleyball

Introduction

  • fast-paced team sport invented in 1895 by William G. Morgan in Holyoke, Massachusetts.
  • played best-of-3 with 6 players on each team.
  • First to 2 sets are played to 25 points with the third match played to 15 points.

Basic Rules

  • Maximum of Three Hits Per Side
  • No Catching, Holding, or Throwing
  • Rally-Point Scoring & Rotation

Positions

  • Setter: Takes the second contact and delivers the ball to hitters.
  • Spiker (Outside / Opposite / Middle): Hits the ball over the net.
  • Libero: Specializes in digging, and serve-receive passing.
